Pawprint

shelter data research club

Why do some shelter animals wait so long for a home?

Ask a question about shelter stays and outcomes. Our little research team digs through twelve years of real intake and outcome records at the same time, then writes you a report where every number links to the query that found it. Made for shelter volunteers, staff and rescue coordinators deciding which animals need extra help, and for adopters curious who gets overlooked.

a full report takes one to three minutes. the dashed one is vague on purpose! after a report, ask a follow-up like "what about cats?" and the team remembers what you were talking about.

  1. step 1

    The Clarifier listens

    It reads your question with the conversation so far. Too vague? It asks you one thing. Otherwise it writes the team a clear brief.

  2. step 2

    The Lead plans

    It splits the brief into 2 to 4 angles. If the findings leave a gap, it sends another round, up to three.

  3. step 3

    Researchers dig together

    Each takes one angle and writes its own SQL, all at once, starting fresh with only its task.

  4. step 4

    The Writer reports

    Every number cites its query. A number that doesn't match its query's result goes back for a rewrite.
